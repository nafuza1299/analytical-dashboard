# Popover browser checks

1. Run `npm run dev` and open the demo's **Floating content** card.
2. Click **Filters**; confirm a bordered surface panel opens below the trigger and focus enters its form control.
3. Confirm it closes when clicking Filters again, clicking outside, pressing Escape, and submitting **Apply filters**.
4. Resize or position near a viewport edge and confirm the panel flips or shifts without clipping. Repeat in light and dark themes.
